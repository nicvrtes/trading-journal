ICC checklist

Indication, correction, continuation. Indication is a strong 1-hour or 4-hour push that closes beyond an older swing. Correction is a pullback that can sweep a high or low but does not close beyond the structure point. Continuation is a break of that level, a retest, then an engulfing candle that has closed on the 15-minute or 5-minute chart. A candle that is still forming does not count.
